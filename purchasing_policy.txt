LAKEVIEW FOODS - PURCHASING POLICY 

1. Pay only vendors listed in approved_vendors.txt.
2. Every invoice must reference a purchase order (PO) number.
3. Invoices over $5,000 must be approved by a VP (the approver field must show "VP").
4. The invoice total must equal quantity x unit price.
5. Duplicate invoices (same vendor, quantity, unit price and total, with a different invoice number) must be held, not paid.

Required output from the reviewer:
- A table of flagged invoices with the rule each one breaks.
- Draft emails to the affected vendors. Do not send anything.
